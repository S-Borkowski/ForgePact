# ForgePact 1.3.10

Works together with Hero Siege Item Editor 2.15.3.

## What's new

- **Tyrant's Crown and Headhunter can now drop in the game.** Rare and champion monsters
  have a 1 in 2000 chance, Ancients 1 in 200, and after 1500 rare-tier kills without a
  drop the next one is guaranteed. The two items take turns. They drop as SS-tier Unholy
  items with everything set up, so loot filters show them. No editor needed; the plugin
  recognises them on every load.
- **Tier for forged items.** A forged item can now carry a Tier letter (C to SS). The
  Item Editor 2.15.3 offers it under Appearance; the built-in Tyrant's Crown and
  Headhunter are SS.
- **Panel:** the Monster Rarity card moved up, above Map Reveal.
- No changes to Special Content, drops, density or any other ForgePact feature.

## How to update

Download the zip, unzip it, run ForgePact and press **Install** once.

Use ForgePact only with an offline / EAC-disabled copy of Hero Siege.
